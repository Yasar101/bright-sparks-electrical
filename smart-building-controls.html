Unified Building Intelligence

What our smart building systems control.

Instead of separate siloed systems for heating, lighting, and ventilation, Bright Sparks integrates all commercial building services into one coordinated platform.

01 / CLIMATE & HVAC

Heating, Cooling & Heat Pumps

Interlocked temperature control preventing simultaneous heating and cooling. Autonomous setbacks triggered when rooms or entire zones are vacated.

02 / LIGHTING INTEGRATION

DALI-2 Architectural & High-Bay Lighting

Addressable lighting scenes, circadian daylight tracking, presence activation, and automated corridor step-down dimming tailored to working hours.

03 / VENTILATION

Demand-Controlled Ventilation (DCV)

Air handling unit (AHU) fan speed modulated directly by indoor air quality (CO2, VOC, humidity) sensors, cutting ventilation heating losses.

04 / SOLAR & SHADING

Automated Facade Blinds & Solar Shading

Motorized commercial louvres and internal blinds automatically adjust based on sun position to harvest solar heat in winter and prevent summer overheating.

05 / ACCESS & SECURITY

Access Control & Building Arming

Fob/keypad access tied directly to building state. Setting the intruder alarm automatically transitions the entire building to deep setback and cuts non-essential power.

06 / LOAD SHEDDING

Sub-Metering & Peak Load Shedding

Real-time circuit power monitoring. Automated shedding of secondary loads (water heaters, EV chargers) during peak network tariff thresholds.

Controls Q&A

Smart controls questions.

Insights for facilities managers, building surveyors, and operations directors.

What happens if our commercial internet connection drops?

Your building automation continues functioning perfectly without interruption. All control algorithms, heating schedules, lighting scenes, and sensor logic execute locally on the Loxone Miniserver's onboard edge processor. Internet connectivity is only required for remote mobile app access and external weather forecasts.

Can this replace an existing failing BMS like Trend or Johnson Controls?

Yes. We routinely retrofit and modernise legacy BMS systems. We can reuse existing field sensor cabling and valve/damper actuators where sound, replacing obsolete out-of-support central controllers with a modern Loxone control architecture at a fraction of the cost of a legacy BMS replacement.

How easy is it for internal facilities staff to adjust temperatures or schedules?

Extremely simple. The system provides an intuitive web interface and mobile app. Authorised facilities managers can adjust room temperatures, override schedules for special events, or review energy consumption with just a few clicks—no specialist programming software required.

Can the system control multiple commercial sites from one login?

Yes. Loxone provides a centralised multi-site management dashboard allowing property directors and estate managers to oversee heating status, energy consumption, and fault alarms across multiple commercial buildings simultaneously.
