AV Questions

Commercial AV FAQs.

Clear guidance for IT managers, facilities directors, and office managers.

What is the difference between consumer TVs and commercial displays?

Domestic consumer televisions are designed to run 4–6 hours per day, have reflective screens, lack RS-232/IP control, and void their warranties when installed in commercial premises. Commercial displays feature anti-glare coatings, commercial warranties (typically 3 years 16/7 or 24/7 operation), commercial thermal management, and failover input switching.

Can our staff share screens wirelessly without installing apps?

Yes. We install native wireless presentation gateways supporting Apple AirPlay, Google Cast, and Miracast, allowing visitors and staff to mirror their laptops or tablets securely onto the main display without needing software downloads or USB dongles.

How does the Loxone integration automate energy shutoff?

The Loxone Miniserver monitors room presence via acoustic and PIR multi-sensors. When the meeting ends and the room remains empty for 10 minutes, Loxone sends automated IP/RS-232 shutdown commands to the displays and disconnects non-essential mains circuits to amplifiers and video bars, completely eliminating vampire phantom loads overnight.

Can you complete the installation over a weekend?

Yes. To ensure your executive boardrooms and meeting spaces remain available for daily operations, our commercial installation team routinely performs wall mounting, core drilling, cabling, and acoustic commissioning over weekend windows.
