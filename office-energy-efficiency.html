The Workplace Dilemma

Why traditional office building controls fail modern hybrid work.

With modern hybrid working patterns, office occupancy varies dramatically day-to-day. Static BMS schedules heat, cool, and light empty desks and vacant meeting rooms continuously.

The Conventional Office Problem

  • Rigid HVAC timers: Boilers and chillers fire from 06:00 to 19:00 regardless of whether floors are at 10% or 100% capacity
  • Heating and cooling conflicts: Perimeter fan coil units heating while core ceiling cassettes blast air conditioning
  • Continuous lighting: Lights burning all day across open-plan floors with abundant natural perimeter daylight
  • Meeting room waste: Boardrooms left with AV displays, air conditioning, and lights running hours after meetings conclude
  • Vampire plug loads: Water chillers, boiling taps, screens, and printers drawing significant baseload power all night

The Bright Sparks Smart Office Approach

  • True presence-linked setbacks: HVAC automatically steps back 2°C–3°C if a zone or floor remains unoccupied for 20 minutes
  • Deadband interlocking: Intelligent software interlocking prevents heating and cooling from ever running simultaneously in the same thermal zone
  • Daylight harvesting: Automated DALI dimming smooths artificial lighting in response to incoming natural daylight
  • One-touch meeting room power: AV, presentation displays, and climate activate on arrival and shut down completely upon departure
  • Scheduled circuit isolation: Non-essential plug loads automatically isolate outside core business hours

Office Guidance

Office efficiency FAQs.

Answers to common questions regarding MEES compliance, office disruption, and tenant sub-metering.

How do smart controls help our commercial EPC rating?

Under the UK Non-Domestic EPC methodology (SBEM), commercial building automation and control systems (BACS) receive significant credit. Upgrading lighting to high-efficacy LED with daylight harvesting, combined with automated HVAC setbacks and zone controls, routinely elevates commercial office buildings by one or two EPC bands (e.g. from Band D to Band B), safeguarding property marketability and MEES compliance.

Can you work around our office working hours?

Yes. We regularly execute commercial office electrical works out-of-hours (from 17:30 onwards or on weekends). Desks, monitors, and paperwork remain clean and undisturbed, allowing your team to work normally during standard office hours.

Can existing AC systems like Daikin or Mitsubishi integrate with Loxone?

Yes. We interface with commercial VRF and split systems using dedicated gateway protocols (such as Modbus, BACnet, or dedicated manufacturer interfaces like Intesis). This gives full digital control over setpoints, operating modes, fan speeds, and occupancy interlocks without voiding AC manufacturer warranties.

How does tenant recharge work with sub-metering?

We install MID-approved digital meters on each tenant distribution board. Data is gathered centrally by the Loxone Miniserver and can be automatically compiled into transparent monthly recharge reports, eliminating tenant disputes over estimated service charge utility apportionments.
