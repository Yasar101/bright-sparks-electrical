Technical Q&A

Loxone monitoring questions.

Answers to common questions from commercial facilities managers, electrical duty holders, and operations directors.

Does installation require a complete building power shutdown?

In most commercial installations, no major shutdown is required. We use high-precision split-core current transformers (CTs) that clamp safely around existing incoming and sub-circuit phase conductors. If a brief isolator opening is required for voltage reference connections, we schedule this during your standard out-of-hours maintenance window to eliminate operational disruption.

Are there mandatory monthly cloud subscription charges?

No. Unlike proprietary metering SaaS platforms that charge £30–£80 per meter per month, the Loxone Miniserver processes and stores operational data locally on dedicated edge storage. You own the hardware, the software, and your data permanently with zero mandatory recurring licensing fees.

Can Loxone automatically switch loads off, or is it read-only?

Loxone is a comprehensive automation controller, not merely a passive monitor. When integrated with contactors, smart circuit breakers, or DALI lighting, it can execute automated actions: shutting down non-essential lighting if an area has been vacant for 15 minutes, disabling water heaters overnight, or shedding secondary cooling during peak network tariff windows.

Can the monitoring data integrate with our corporate ESG reporting?

Yes. All circuit data can be exported automatically via CSV, REST API, or integrated into corporate reporting dashboards to supply audit-ready Scope 1 and Scope 2 energy data for SECR, ESOS, and BREEAM compliance.
