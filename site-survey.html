Transparent Methodology

What happens during a Bright Sparks commercial survey.

Our surveys are non-disruptive, highly technical investigations conducted by senior commercial electrical engineers—not junior sales reps with generic checklists.

1. Desktop & Utility Data Review

Before arriving on site, we analyse your facility's recent half-hourly (HH) data, electrical single-line diagrams, and utility supply tariffs.

  • Identify overnight and weekend baseload power draw
  • Detect maximum demand spikes and kVA capacity breaches
  • Evaluate current power factor and reactive power penalty charges
  • Determine operational hours vs. unoccupied energy burn

2. On-Site Physical Technical Inspection

Our engineers perform a methodical physical walk-through of your commercial premises, plant rooms, and distribution boards.

  • Visual and thermal imaging inspection of main intake switchgear
  • Survey of existing lighting fixtures, controls, and lux levels
  • Audit of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning controls
  • Assessment of high-load plant, refrigeration, motors, and compressors

3. Baseload & Waste Quantification

We calculate the exact financial cost of each identified inefficiency based on your current unit electricity tariff.

  • Cost of unoccupied lighting in racking or offices
  • Cost of conflicting heating and cooling runtimes
  • Annual penalty costs incurred from poor power factor
  • Estimate of savings from Loxone automation and setback scheduling

4. Prioritised Energy-Saving Action Plan

A comprehensive engineering and financial deliverable presented directly to your management team.

  • Prioritised list of recommendations ranked by payback speed
  • Exact capital investment required (turnkey, fully costed)
  • Projected annual kWh reduction and financial savings (£/yr)
  • Board-level business case documentation and capital allowance guidance

Survey Questions

Frequently asked survey questions.

Clear answers about survey logistics, deliverables, and commercial confidentiality.

How long does the on-site physical survey take?

For a typical 20,000 to 60,000 sq ft warehouse or commercial office building, the physical on-site audit takes between 2 to 4 hours. Our engineers work quietly and respectfully in the background, requiring only accompanied access to plant rooms and main electrical intake switchrooms. Your normal business operations are not disrupted.

What information should we prepare prior to the site visit?

To extract maximum value from the survey, it is helpful (though not strictly required) to provide: 1) A copy of your most recent commercial electricity bill (showing half-hourly data or peak kVA demand); 2) Facility floor plans or layout drawings if available; 3) Standard operating hours and shift patterns.

Are we obligated to proceed with the recommended upgrades?

No. You are under no obligation to proceed with any installation. The Energy-Saving Action Plan is your property to review with your facilities team, property directors, and board. You can choose to implement all measures, phase them over several budget years, or execute only the fastest-payback items.

Do you sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) before visiting?

Yes. We regularly work in confidential manufacturing, pharmaceutical, logistics, and data-sensitive office environments. We are happy to execute your standard mutual NDA prior to attending site or reviewing your energy and electrical data.
