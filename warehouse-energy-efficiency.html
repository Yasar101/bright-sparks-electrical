The Commercial Reality

Where warehouses haemorrhage operational expenditure.

Logistics buildings have vast floor plates and high ceilings, making them notoriously vulnerable to unmanaged baseload energy waste.

Common Warehouse Energy Inefficiencies

  • Continuous high-bay lighting: 250W–400W fixtures burning 24/7 across empty racking aisles
  • Loading dock door heater waste: Radiant heaters running continuously while dock doors remain wide open
  • Unmanaged mezzanine & pack-bench heating: Convectors and radiant tubes left running overnight
  • Poor power factor from motors: Inductive loads from conveyor drives, sorting systems, and compactor pumps incurring penalty charges
  • Refrigeration compressor drift: Cold store evaporator coils running unmonitored with degraded defrost cycles

The Bright Sparks Engineered Solution

  • Zoned narrow-beam aisle detection: High-performance PIR sensors activate lighting only when forklifts or pickers enter an aisle
  • Dock door interlock controls: Loxone sensors automatically isolate radiant heaters the instant dock leveller doors are open or bays are unoccupied
  • Daylight harvesting via skylights: Photocells automatically dim lighting rows near rooflights when natural daylight exceeds required lux levels
  • Power factor correction (PFC): Behind-the-meter capacitor banks eliminate reactive power charges on motor supplies
  • Sub-metered cold store tracking: 24/7 temperature and compressor electrical load monitoring with automated freeze alerts

Logistics Guidance

Warehouse questions answered.

Practical details on installation staging, shift disruption, and health & safety compliance.

Can installation take place without stopping our warehouse operations?

Yes. We regularly work in live 24/7 logistics environments. We execute high-level cabling and luminaire changeovers aisle-by-aisle or during scheduled weekend windows, cordoning off single work areas so your picking, packing, and dispatch operations continue uninterrupted.

What mounting heights can your aisle motion sensors handle?

We deploy specialised high-mount optics engineered specifically for narrow racking corridors up to 16 metres high. The narrow rectangular detection pattern ensures sensors trigger only when a vehicle or operative enters that specific aisle, preventing cross-aisle false triggering.

Do you handle the emergency lighting compliance in warehouses?

Yes. We design and install BS 5266-compliant commercial emergency lighting systems, including automated self-testing DALI emergency luminaires that eliminate manual monthly key-switch testing across extensive warehouse footprints.

How quickly can you survey our warehouse facility?

We can typically carry out a site survey within 3 to 5 business days across the North West and nationwide. Within one week of the site audit, we present your comprehensive Energy-Saving Action Plan with exact equipment counts, lux simulations, and payback modelling.
