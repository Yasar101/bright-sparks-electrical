The Business Case

Why commercial lighting upgrades deliver unmatched ROI.

Lighting typically accounts for 25% to 40% of a commercial building's total electricity bill. Legacy fixtures are not only energy-inefficient but require continuous, expensive maintenance.

Legacy Commercial Lighting (Metal Halide / Fluorescent T8/T5)

  • Poor luminous efficacy (typically 50–80 lm/W vs 160+ lm/W for modern LED)
  • Significant heat generation placing additional load on building air conditioning
  • Frequent lamp failures requiring elevated access platforms and ongoing re-lamping costs
  • Slow strike times (metal halide takes 5–15 mins to restrike, preventing sensor controls)
  • Degraded lumen output and yellowing colour temperature over time

Bright Sparks Commercial LED Retrofits

  • Premium commercial optics delivering 160–180 lumens per watt
  • Instant-on, instant-off switching with zero restrike delays or warm-up time
  • 50,000 to 100,000-hour rated lifespan (L80B10) virtually eliminating maintenance
  • Integrated DALI-2 digital dimming, daylight harvesting, and presence stepdown
  • Turnkey installation: MEWP access, out-of-hours delivery, and WEEE disposal

Lighting FAQs

Commercial lighting questions.

Answers regarding photometric design, disruption, and maintenance guarantees.

Do you provide a formal lighting design simulation before installation?

Yes. Before any physical installation commences, our lighting engineers produce a full 3D DIALux or Relux photometric calculation model using your facility's exact dimensions, ceiling heights, and surface reflectances. This proves that required CIBSE lux levels and uniformity targets will be achieved across every working zone.

What warranty is provided on the commercial LED fixtures?

We specify exclusively tier-one commercial grade LED luminaires and drivers (such as Tridonic, Osram, or Philips OEM components) featuring a comprehensive 5-year manufacturer warranty, with operational lifespans typically rated at 50,000 to 100,000 hours (L80B10).

How is our old lighting disposed of?

Bright Sparks handles the safe removal, segregation, and compliant disposal of all legacy fittings and lamps under the UK Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (WEEE) regulations. Fluorescent tubes containing mercury are safely recycled with full hazardous waste transfer notes provided.

Can we integrate emergency lighting into the same LED fixtures?

Yes. We regularly supply and install combined luminaires with integral emergency battery packs and DALI-2 addressability. This avoids installing separate unsightly emergency bulkheads and ensures full BS 5266 emergency illumination along escape routes.
