Commercial Insights

Frequently asked questions.

Essential information for facilities managers, property directors, and business operators evaluating commercial energy surveys, Loxone controls, and electrical upgrades.

What occurs during a commercial energy efficiency site survey?

Our qualified commercial electrical engineers conduct a non-disruptive, comprehensive physical and technical inspection of your facility. We examine your main intake, distribution boards, lighting circuits, HVAC controls, and baseload power draw. We cross-reference this with your half-hourly meter data to uncover operational waste (such as unoccupied equipment runtimes or conflicting heating/cooling). We then deliver a prioritised Energy-Saving Action Plan with estimated kWh reductions, capital requirements, and payback timelines.

How does Loxone energy monitoring differ from standard sub-meters?

Traditional sub-meters merely record kilowatt-hours passively for retrospective utility billing. Loxone energy monitoring is an active, intelligent building ecosystem. It measures real-time circuit loads every second, correlates consumption with space occupancy and environmental sensors, and can automatically take protective actions—such as shutting down non-critical circuits during peak tariff periods or sending instant alerts if equipment runs outside scheduled operating hours.

Why work with Bright Sparks rather than separate consultants and electricians?

Most businesses face a frustrating disconnect between software consultants who produce advisory reports but don't install hardware, and traditional electricians who wire circuits but lack building automation programming skills. Bright Sparks eliminates this friction. We are fully accredited commercial electrical contractors AND certified Loxone automation specialists. We survey, design, wire, program, commission, and verify savings as a single accountable partner.

What typical payback period can we expect on commercial controls and lighting?

Most commercial LED lighting retrofits and Loxone smart control installations achieve full capital payback within 12 to 24 months. Lighting retrofits deliver immediate 50–70% reductions in lighting power, while smart controls permanently eliminate out-of-hours heating, ventilation, and baseload waste. We provide rigorous financial modelling in your Action Plan to assist your board with capital approval.

Can you support our corporate ESG and BREEAM sustainability targets?

Yes. Granular Loxone sub-metering and verified energy reductions provide verifiable, audit-grade data required for Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR), Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions documentation, EPC rating enhancements, and BREEAM building energy management credits.

Do you carry out commercial electrical compliance and statutory EICRs?

Yes. Commercial electrical contracting and compliance are core to our business. We carry out 5-year periodic Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICRs), fixed-wire inspection and testing, thermal imaging surveys, 3-phase board replacements, and remedial safety repairs to ensure your building complies fully with BS 7671 and insurer requirements.
